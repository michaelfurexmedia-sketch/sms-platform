# Relayline design system

Temporary brand, defined only in src/lib/config.ts. Product: self-service SMS for a first-time business owner, with a connected platform-owner review portal.

Public surfaces persuade through a working conversation and exact prepaid pricing. Workspace and Admin surfaces prioritize readable records and one next action.

Palette: Paper #f7f6f2, White #ffffff, Ink #182321, Cobalt #2454e6, Mist #e9ece5, Muted #64706b. Bricolage Grotesque display; Geist body. Tabular numeric data. Equal gutters and capped marketing width. Quiet borders in work surfaces, flat filled product demonstration on the homepage. No invented testimonials or traction.

Signature: a campaign becomes a reply without leaving the hero. Compose and conversation are two states of the same live demonstration, mirrored in the actual inbox.

Plan review: avoided a generic feature-card grid; the first screen pairs one strong two-line promise with a working conversation. Pricing uses one selector and expandable comparison, while onboarding distinguishes email, number and verification.

Skills read and applied: design-taste-frontend (composition and product demonstration), frontend-design (hierarchy and copy), design-system (three-layer tokens), ui-styling (responsive composition), ui-ux-pro-max (accessibility and navigation), impeccable (critique and empty states), hallmark (identity and restrained ornament). marketing-design was read and its frontend routing applied; no raster marketing assets are needed. reverse-engineer-anything informed evidence boundaries for public references. chrome-devtools was read; its MCP tools were unavailable, so available browser tooling is used. Companion scripts and reference resources for the user-installed skills were inaccessible; their automated gates are not claimed as run.
