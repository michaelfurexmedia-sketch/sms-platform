# Public reference research

- [Textla public website](https://www.textla.com/): messaging product positioning; no private dashboard access claimed.
- [Campaign guide](https://help.textla.com/en/articles/6050300-how-to-send-an-sms-campaign): sender selection, groups/tags, personalization with fallback, preview and immediate/future/recurring scheduling.
- [Contact statuses](https://help.textla.com/en/articles/9265883-textla-contact-statuses): Subscribed, Lead, Invalid and Opted out; individual Lead replies and campaign exclusions.
- [Ijadi homepage](https://ijadi.io/): concrete journey demonstration, headline hierarchy and primary/secondary calls to action informed an original messaging presentation.

The references establish public behavior, not private backend implementation. Supplied notes about marketing/app frameworks and historical Twilio setup are not evidence of current private infrastructure. This prototype requests no customer provider credentials and installs no third-party analytics or support scripts.
